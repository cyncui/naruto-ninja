# Design context

Audience: the user and Naruto fans experimenting with a camera-based jutsu playground.
Primary task: choose a technique, learn or practice its hand-seal sequence, and preview a live camera effect.
Personality: faithful NERV monitor homage from Neon Genesis Evangelion (black field, amber phosphor plates, green grid, seven-segment readouts, Japanese labels). User explicitly rejects generic AI-generated visual patterns. See DESIGN.md.
Constraints: lightweight browser app; camera-first on desktop and phone; honest experimental tracking and personal calibration. No implication that symbolic research is a trained vision model.
