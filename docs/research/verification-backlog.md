Scene references reported by a secondary page should stay labelled as reported until inspected. Useful starting references already exist in variant `sourceLocator` and `notes` fields. Where only a technique debut is known, do not assume it contains the whole activation sequence.

For training, split recordings by performer and session. Count failed hand detections as failures; do not discard them and present the surviving-frame classification score as end-to-end accuracy. Camera transformations and confidence/timing thresholds are separate engineering decisions and should be measured on the target devices.
