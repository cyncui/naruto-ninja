# Catalogue research coverage

Research date: October 9, 2026. The canonical development data is `../data/naruto-catalogue.json`. This ledger describes this research pass, not a completeness guarantee.

## General techniques

- Research snapshot: 2026-10-09. Ordered sequences are source-reported, not primary verified.
- Narutopedia search excerpts were accessible, direct pages blocked. Original scene inspection is required before presenting sequences as canon-verified tutorials.
- Names, alias normalization and ordered sequences are factual metadata; prose here is original concise synthesis.
- No universal mapping exists from a single seal to one power: Tiger and Clone Seal collide across techniques.
- The twelve basic seal names and romaji are recorded; no undocumented pose geometry or handedness is fabricated.
- Novel one-handed clone variants and movie Shrine Seal poses remain outside initial recognizer coverage.
- All effectSuggestion values are proposed app designs, not source assertions.

## Elemental techniques

- Scope: 28 Naruto/Shippuden-era elemental techniques across Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Lightning, Wood and Ice. No Boruto-only, movie-only or game-only techniques were added to fill a quota.
- All ordered seal claims are secondary_reported or unresolved. No primary panel or complete original activation footage was inspected, and no record is primary_verified.
- Search returned substantial indexed Narutopedia article content but direct retrieval was restricted. access is therefore search_excerpt even when an index exposed most of an article.
- SourceLocator values point to the actual accessed secondary field. Any manga/episode locator is explicitly described as that reference's citation, not primary verification.
- Most infobox lists combine media and do not identify their scene. medium unspecified deliberately preserves that uncertainty.
- Rabbit is normalized to hare; aliases and seal lists are factual normalization, not new gesture geometry. The Yang Water seal remains separate.
- Sequences contain explicit actions where the source lists them. null means unresolved; [] appears only for the explicitly reported seal-free Chidori variant.
- Element and character/clan associations are not exclusivity rules. Repeated single signs cannot identify a unique power without technique context.
- Training/recognition geometry for special signs still requires verified visual reference. Secondary prose is insufficient to train a robust pose model.
- AR effectSuggestion fields are original product ideas rather than claims of canonical activation or complete fictional powers.
- Wind Gale Palm has Naruto/Shippuden provenance but its current mixed-era infobox sequence lacks a verified original scene; investigate before presenting it as period-specific canon.

## Clan techniques

- 20 clan-associated techniques/abilities spanning Nara, Yamanaka, Akimichi, Inuzuka, Aburame, Hozuki, Uchiha, Hyuga, Kaguya, and Uzumaki. This is a bounded catalogue, not every franchise clan or technique.
- All encoded sequences are secondary-reported; none is promoted to primary_verified by an official article that only confirms the effect or clan.
- Missing sequence fields are null/unresolved, never proof of no seals. Empty arrays are reserved for an explicit source statement of no seals.
- Technique-specific Yamanaka classes are symbolic catalogue IDs, not sufficient geometry for a detector.
- Many secondary infoboxes do not specify the medium for their hand-seal field; those variants retain unspecified instead of treating a manga debut as sequence provenance.
- Finger Gun and all effect suggestions are separate from the zodiac-seal vocabulary. Effect suggestions are product proposals, not additional canon assertions.
- The secondary Multi-Size reference may duplicate Narutopedia; it is not independent corroboration.
- Hand Seal geometry notes and infobox sequence labels can differ, notably Shikamaru Bird and Akimichi. Visual-reference audit remains necessary before training.

## Supplementary coverage

- Ten supplementary records include Rasengan, Uzumaki sealing with repeated Dog, anime-only Fuma and Hoshigakure techniques, a distinct Hyuga special seal, and a movie-specific Tiger activation.
- Official articles support broad concepts only. Exact symbolic triggers remain secondary-reported.
- Boruto Kote one-handed elemental controls and complete movie-specific spoken-seal vocabularies are recorded in the coverage backlog rather than conflated with the twelve standard signs.

## Cross-check corrections

- Independently compared all 44 Water Dragon steps; Yang Water occurs at one-based positions 21 and 41.
- Earth Wall snake-ending alternative retains the secondary citation to Boruto episode 181.
- Jin and Yang Water share one canonical ID; similarly named movie invocations remain distinct.
- Shadow Sewing retains the infobox Bird label with a discrepancy note; no new ordered sequence was invented by substituting Shikamaru's Bird variant.
- No-seal abilities are explicit source claims. An omitted infobox field is not proof of no seals.
- Reanimation cancellation and contract severing are separate catalogue entries. Kabuto's deceptive release explanation is not accepted as a sequence.
